Self-verification tool, not certification. This tracker is a working aid for recording your own EN71-1 physical & mechanical testing as part of a UKCA/CE technical file. It does not replace professional advice, and it is not a substitute for third-party test house certification where that is required. Clause references are EN71-1 section numbers, shown for cross-referencing only. If you are unsure whether your toy meets requirements, consult your local Trading Standards body or an accredited toy-testing laboratory — responsibility for product safety and compliance rests with the manufacturer.

Data in this tool is saved automatically to this artifact. Use Backup occasionally to keep a downloaded copy, and Print record to save a finished record into your technical file.

My details

Your business details and the answers that are the same on every toy. Filled into any blank field on every record, old and new. These are stored with your data — never in the app file — so the app itself stays safe to host publicly.

A details file is a small private copy of just this page — handy for setting up a new device in one tap. Keep it out of any public repository.

Cloud backup

Keeps one copy of everything — records, materials, batches, photos and certificates — in a private GitHub repository, so every device shares the same data and every save is kept as a dated version you can go back to.
